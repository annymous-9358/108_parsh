Lord Parshvanath is the 23rd Tirthankara of the Jain tradition. Just remembering him awakens feelings of compassion, love, devotion and peace in the hearts of the devotees. In the present times, like Kalpavriksha, the idols and pilgrimage places of Lord Shri Parshvanath are worshiped with immense devotion all over India.

After attaining Samyaktva, Lord Parshvanath's soul assumed ten previous incarnations. In the life of Suvarnabahu, he took the vow of Tirthankar Naamkarma, and in his last Devbhava, he continued to reside in the form of a god in the heavenly plane called Pranata.

On Phalgun Vad Chauth, his Chyavan Kalyanak took place in Vishakha Nakshatra. He was born in the Ikshvaku dynasty and Kashyap gotra in the city of Varanasi as the son of King Ashwasen and Queen Vama Devi. After being in the womb for nine months and six days, his birth was celebrated as Kalyanak in Vishakha Nakshatra on Magasar Vad Dashami. The complexion of his body was blue and the stigma of a snake, and his physical height was nine cubits.

On Magasar Vad Baras, in Anuradha Nakshatra, Lord Parshvanath along with three hundred princes took initiation along with Chhath penance while sitting in a palanquin named Vishala. He was first blessed with Kheer at the hands of Dhanya Kumar in Kopkat Nagar.

After eighty-four days of Chhadmastha Sadhana, while performing the eighth penance, on Phalgun Vad Chauth, in Vishakha Nakshatra, he attained enlightenment under the Dhataki tree in the city of Varanasi. Parsvayaksha and Padmavati Devi became their ruling deities and ruling goddesses.

The subject of the first sermon of Lord Parshvanath was the description of the reasons for the twelve fasts and Karmabandha. Under his rule, there were sixteen thousand monks and thirty-eight thousand Sadhvis including Pushpchula, one lakh sixty-four thousand Shravaks and three lakh seventy-seven thousand Shravikas. Among them, there were one thousand Kevalgyani, seven hundred fifty Manaparyavgyani, fourteen hundred Avadhigyani, three hundred and fourteen Purvadhars, one thousand Vaikriyalabdhidhars and six hundred Vadi.

Lord Parshvanath was hundred years old. On Shravan Sud Eighth, in Vishakha Nakshatra, he attained Nirvana along with thirty-three sages in Kayotsarga posture while performing Masakshaman penance at Sammed Shikhar Tirtha. His first Ganadhar was Shri Shubhadutta. The difference between Lord Parshvanath and Lord Mahavir is considered to be two hundred and fifty years.

Even today, devotees of Lord Parshvanath enjoy his darshan and worship at 108 Parshvanath Tirthas across India, and his compassionate image provides spiritual peace to the devotees.
